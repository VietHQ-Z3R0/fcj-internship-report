This section will list and introduce the blogs you have posted to [AWS Study Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/awsstudygroupfcj). For example:

###  [Blog 1 - Amazon S3 Lifecycle – 22/10/2026](3.1-Blog1/)
Amazon S3 Lifecycle provides automated storage management capabilities, allowing organizations to define rules for transitioning or deleting objects based on their age and storage requirements. This helps reduce unnecessary storage costs by automatically managing temporary uploads, abandoned files, and outdated data without requiring constant manual intervention.

###  [Blog 2 - AWS Savings Plans – 28/10/2026](3.2-Blog2/)
AWS Savings Plans provide a flexible pricing model that allows organizations to reduce compute costs by committing to a consistent hourly usage amount over a one- or three-year period. In exchange, eligible compute usage receives discounted pricing compared to On-Demand rates. This offers an opportunity to optimize long-term infrastructure expenses while maintaining flexibility in how compute resources are used.
